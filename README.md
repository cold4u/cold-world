# 🪐 Cold World - 3D Planet Delivery Game

> *"It's a small planet, but someone's gotta make the deliveries."*

**Hosted and maintained by [cold4u](https://github.com/cold4u)**  
🎮 **Play Online**: [https://cold4u.github.io/cold-world/](https://cold4u.github.io/cold-world/)

A 3D spherical low-poly planet delivery adventure built with Three.js and Svelte.

## 🌟 Features
- **Spherical Surface Gravity**: Full 360° navigation where your character always aligns perpendicularly to the planet's curvature.
- **7 Detailed Biomes**:
  - Main Square (Town Plaza, Flower Stalls, Fountain)
  - Red Cliff House (The Boss's Executive Villa)
  - Mount Temple (Torii Gate, Cherry Blossoms, Mountain Shrine)
  - Enchanted Forest & Cave (Pine trees, Campfire, Cave entrance)
  - Smelly Falls (Waterfall canyon, Wooden dock)
  - Capital Corp (Futuristic Tech Laboratories)
  - Tropical Beach (Palm trees, Sand shores, Ocean pier)
- **Living Wildlife & World Inhabitants (19 Unique Characters - Zero Clones)**:
  - **Every Citizen is Unique**: 19 distinct character models across all 7 biomes—each with individual voices, dialogue, and roles (Arthur the Courier, Chloe the Florist, Boss Reginald, Caveman Grog, Captain Steve, Chef Pierre, Dave the Musician, Mountain Guy, Grandma Etsu, Dr. Frebi, Dr. Frieb, Chief Mechanic Otto, Patrolman Tengo, Technician Getave, and Pale Man).
  - **Prominent & Interactive Children**:
    - *Toby the Kid (`threekid`)*: Right on the Town Plaza main promenade with an active talk prompt icon, practicing sprints to be the fastest courier on the planet.
    - *Scout Oliver (`scout`)*: Visible in the forest clearing near the campfire and Ranger Lodge, earning her planetary exploration badge and looking out for her dad, Chef Pierre.
  - **Glitch-Free Natural Wildlife**:
    - *Wild Red Fox*: Resting peacefully in the Enchanted Forest meadow with authentic wild idle animation (no human rotation glitches).
    - *Wise Horned Owl*: Perched on the forest crag overlooking the canyon with natural breathing animation.
  - **Smooth Sky Flight Flocks**: Clean, soaring bird flocks gliding smoothly along orbital planetary air currents without GPU particle collision glitches.
- **World Expansion & Infrastructure**:
  - **Cold World Express Delivery Vans**: Fleet of 5 branded, low-poly delivery vans across the planet (Town Plaza Express #01, Capital Heavy Cargo #04, Seaside Beach Runner #07, Mountain High Expedition #03, Canyon Trail Camper #05) complete with chassis, rubber tires, silver hubcaps, windshields, headlights with emissive glow, taillights, roof parcel racks, and packages.
  - **5 Architectural Landmarks**:
    - *Town Postal Station & Mailbox*: Central depot with gabled roof, front porch awning, warm lit windows, and iconic red postal drop box.
    - *Seaside Beacon Lighthouse*: Red-and-white nautical beacon tower, stone sea-wall jetty foundation, revolving lantern room, and observation gallery.
    - *Sacred Zen Mountain Pagoda & Tea Pavilion*: Multi-tiered jade green pagoda roofs, vermilion pillars, glowing shoji paper lanterns, and stone garden bench.
    - *Forester Ranger Lodge & Lookout*: Log cabin styling with moss-shake gabled roof, fieldstone chimney, covered front veranda, and stacked split-wood logs.
    - *Capital Corp Drone Station*: Modular hexagonal tech pod with glowing cyan seam lines, rooftop helipad with hazard chevrons, dual solar panel wings, and comms antenna mast.
  - **4 Paved Road Networks**:
    - *Town Cobblestone Promenade*: Interconnected paving slabs with stone curbs connecting the plaza to the postal station.
    - *Seaside Timber Boardwalk*: Nautical timber plankway extending towards the lighthouse jetty.
    - *Mountain Pilgrim Flagstone Steps*: Carved stone stepping path ascending the peak towards the Zen Pagoda.
    - *Capital Industrial Strip*: Dark asphalt pavement with yellow safety lane stripes leading to the drone hub.
  - **Expanded Handcrafted Forests & Groves (35+ Trees & Flora)**:
    - *The Whispering Pines*: 14 multi-tiered evergreen conifers with red toadstools and mossy boulders.
    - *Sacred Sakura Orchard*: 8 flowering cherry blossom trees with fluffy pastel pink canopies and fallen petal mounds.
    - *Seaside Palm Oasis*: 7 curved tropical palm trees with textured trunks and fan fronds.
    - *Autumn Golden Woods*: 6 golden-amber deciduous trees overlooking Smelly Falls.
- **5 Canonical Delivery Storylines**:
  1. *Falling off the corporate ladder* (Office Worker & The Boss)
  2. *A man who's hiding from something* (Caveman & Flower Lady)
  3. *Scientists & mixed-up deliveries* (Dr. Frieb & Dr. Frebi)
  4. *An offering to the mountain temple* (Old Woman & Mountain Man)
  5. *A note lost at sea* (The Diver & Dave the Musician)
- **Original Audio & Ambiance**:
  - High-fidelity background soundtrack and atmospheric soundscapes.
  - Animal Crossing style speech voice blips with pitch variations.
  - Responsive footstep, jump, and victory sound effects.
- **Customization & UI**:
  - **High-Performance 60 FPS Engine**: Optimized WebGL pipeline with hardware `PCFShadowMap`, 1024px dynamic shadow maps, selective shadow casting, matrix auto-update caching, and real-time live FPS monitor overlay.
  - Complete courier outfit customizer.
  - Delivery checklist log modal.
  - Responsive mobile touch support with on-screen virtual joystick.

## 🕹️ Controls
- **Movement**: `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or Arrow Keys
- **Jump**: `Space`
- **Sprint**: `Shift`
- **Interact / Talk**: `E` or click on nearby citizens
- **Orbit Camera**: Left-click & drag
- **Zoom**: Mouse scroll wheel

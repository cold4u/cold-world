# 🪐 Cold World - 3D Planet Delivery Game

> *"It's a small planet, but someone's gotta make the deliveries."*

**Hosted and maintained by [cold4u](https://github.com/cold4u)**  
🎮 **Play Online**: [https://cold4u.github.io/cold-world/](https://cold4u.github.io/cold-world/)

A 3D spherical low-poly planet delivery adventure built with Three.js and Svelte.

## 🌟 Features
- **Spherical Surface Gravity**: Full 360° navigation where your character always aligns perpendicularly to the planet's curvature.
- **7 Detailed Biomes**:
  - Main Square (Town Plaza, Flower Stalls, Fountain)
  - Red Cliff House (The Boss's Executive Villa)
  - Mount Temple (Torii Gate, Cherry Blossoms, Mountain Shrine)
  - Enchanted Forest & Cave (Pine trees, Campfire, Cave entrance)
  - Smelly Falls (Waterfall canyon, Wooden dock)
  - Capital Corp (Futuristic Tech Laboratories)
  - Tropical Beach (Palm trees, Sand shores, Ocean pier)
- **Living Wildlife & World Inhabitants**:
  - **Animated Animals Across Every Biome**: Playful foxes, kitsunes, and wise horned owls in towns, forests, temples, beaches, and waterfalls.
  - **Interactive Children**: Scouts earning badges, shell-gatherers, mountain climbers, and tech lab apprentices with full speech bubbles and voice blips.
  - **Flocks of Soaring Birds**: Over 90 birds gliding on spherical air currents across the sky.
- **World Expansion & Infrastructure**:
  - **Cold World Express Delivery Vans & Couriers**: Fleet of 5 branded, low-poly delivery vans across the planet with designated courier drivers and full game UI dialogue:
    - *Express #01 & Courier Leo (Town Plaza)*: Royal Blue delivery van and town dispatcher with route updates.
    - *Heavy Cargo #04 & Driver Otto (Capital Corp)*: High-voltage industrial hauler transporting quantum batteries.
    - *Beach Runner #07 & Courier Kai (Seaside)*: Sky Blue surf van delivering coastal mail to the pier.
    - *Expedition #03 & Courier Maya (Mountain)*: High-altitude van preparing for steep switchback climbs.
    - *Camp Camper #05 & Courier Rusty (Smelly Falls)*: Canyon trail camper van sharing roasted campfire snacks.
  - **5 Interactive Architectural Landmarks & Keepers**:
    - *Town Postal Station & Stationmaster Penny*: Gabled blue depot with red drop box and central shipping registry.
    - *Seaside Beacon Lighthouse & Keeper Barnaby*: Nautical beacon tower guiding ships through the starry cosmos.
    - *Sacred Zen Mountain Pagoda & Master Sora*: Jade green pagoda shrine offering peaceful mountain wisdom.
    - *Forester Ranger Lodge & Ranger Rowan*: Woodland log cabin watching over wildlife and forest canopies.
    - *Capital Corp Drone Station & Officer Jax*: Hexagonal drone relay pod with live solar telemetry.
  - **4 Paved Road Networks**:
    - *Town Cobblestone Promenade*: Interconnected paving slabs with stone curbs connecting the plaza to the postal station.
    - *Seaside Timber Boardwalk*: Nautical timber plankway extending towards the lighthouse jetty.
    - *Mountain Pilgrim Flagstone Steps*: Carved stone stepping path ascending the peak towards the Zen Pagoda.
    - *Capital Industrial Strip*: Dark asphalt pavement with yellow safety lane stripes leading to the drone hub.
  - **Expanded Handcrafted Forests & Groves (35+ Trees & Flora)**:
    - *The Whispering Pines*: 14 multi-tiered evergreen conifers with red toadstools and mossy boulders.
    - *Sacred Sakura Orchard*: 8 flowering cherry blossom trees with fluffy pastel pink canopies and fallen petal mounds.
    - *Seaside Palm Oasis*: 7 curved tropical palm trees with textured trunks and fan fronds.
    - *Autumn Golden Woods*: 6 golden-amber deciduous trees overlooking Smelly Falls.
- **5 Canonical Delivery Storylines**:
  1. *Falling off the corporate ladder* (Office Worker & The Boss)
  2. *A man who's hiding from something* (Caveman & Flower Lady)
  3. *Scientists & mixed-up deliveries* (Dr. Frieb & Dr. Frebi)
  4. *An offering to the mountain temple* (Old Woman & Mountain Man)
  5. *A note lost at sea* (The Diver & Dave the Musician)
- **Original Audio & Ambiance**:
  - High-fidelity background soundtrack and atmospheric soundscapes.
  - Animal Crossing style speech voice blips with pitch variations.
  - Responsive footstep, jump, and victory sound effects.
- **Customization & UI**:
  - Complete courier outfit customizer.
  - Delivery checklist log modal.
  - Responsive mobile touch support with on-screen virtual joystick.

## 🕹️ Controls
- **Movement**: `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or Arrow Keys
- **Jump**: `Space`
- **Sprint**: `Shift`
- **Interact / Talk**: `E` or click on nearby citizens
- **Orbit Camera**: Left-click & drag
- **Zoom**: Mouse scroll wheel
